The Poster Machine

Generative Study / Runs in your browser

Part sketchbook, part slot machine. Every poster here is composed live by a few hundred lines of code — palette, geometry and type rolled from a random seed.

No two prints are ever the same, and none can be repeated unless you keep the seed. This is the experiment at the heart of my practice: the machine drafts, the designer decides. Pull the lever and take a print home.

About the designer

Design is my first language — AI is the accent I'm learning to speak in.

I'm Maimna Noor, a graphic designer obsessed with the texture of print in a pixel world. My work lives across brand identity, editorial systems, packaging and posters — anywhere a grid can be bent with intent.

Lately my practice has drifted toward the edge where craft meets computation. I use generative tools to sketch a hundred wild directions before breakfast — then edit ruthlessly with a human eye. The machine drafts; I decide what sings.

Currently — exploring diffusion models for pattern design · open for freelance
